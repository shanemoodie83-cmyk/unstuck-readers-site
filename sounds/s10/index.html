Lesson S10

Dot's Home

Listen with your child before the lesson. Press play, then say the sound together. Keep quick sounds like /t/ and /b/ short, with no "uh" on the end.

New sounds

o_e
as in home

The e at the end makes o say its name: oh.

u_e
as in tube

Says 'yoo', like in tube and cute.

u_e
as in rude

Says 'oo', like in rude and rule. If one sound doesn't make a real word, try the other sound.

e_e
as in these

The e at the end makes e say its name: ee.
