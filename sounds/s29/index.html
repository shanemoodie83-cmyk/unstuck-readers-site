Lesson S29

The Kite in the Gum Tree

Listen with your child before the lesson. Press play, then say the sound together. Keep quick sounds like /t/ and /b/ short, with no "uh" on the end.

New sounds

-ed
as in jumped

Says /t/ after an unvoiced sound: jumped, looked.

-ed
as in played

Says /d/ after a voiced sound: played, climbed.

-ed
as in landed

Says /id/ after t or d: landed, lifted.
